Privacy Policy

CoachYourWard (website study.rekhank.app and the Android app app.rekhank.study). Last updated 24 October 2026.

CoachYourWard is a study app for CBSE Class 10 students. It has no advertising and no analytics, and it never sells or shares personal data.

What is stored

  • On your device: your study plan ticks, test history, exam date, mistake book, chosen subject and which questions you have already seen. These stay in the browser's local storage on that device.
  • If you sign in with Google: your Google account's user ID, name and email address are used by Google Firebase Authentication to sign you in. Your study plan ticks, test history, exam date and mistake book are saved in Google Firebase Cloud Firestore under your user ID so that they sync between your devices.

Signing in is optional. Without it, everything stays on the device.

What is not collected

No location, contacts, photos, microphone, camera or device identifiers. No answers you type in written tests leave your device. No data is used for advertising, and no data is shared with or sold to anyone.

Who processes the data

Sign-in and synced progress are handled by Google Firebase (Google LLC) on our behalf, under Google's terms. Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS). Only the signed-in account can read or write its own progress.

Deleting your data

On a device: use Progress → Reset progress, or clear the app's or site's storage. For synced data: reset progress while signed in, which overwrites the synced copy, or ask the developer (contact below) to delete your account record. Requests are handled within 30 days.

Children

The app is meant for Class 10 students, usually set up with a parent. It does not show ads and does not collect more than is described above.

Contact

Questions or deletion requests: write to the developer email address shown on the app's Google Play Store listing.
